I'm an engineering manager at link:https://www.cloudbees.com/[CloudBees], based in
Toulouse, France. I joined in August 2016 as a software developer, and decided to
change career path and become a manager in May 2019.

I barely code anymore: hands-on as a manager, hands-off on the code. I work on
staying relevant, keeping a genuine curiosity and enough context to ask questions.
I still open pull requests, but for docs or small stuff on the side, so the team
can focus on the real technical work.

Most of what I've worked on is developer tooling: build systems, continuous
integration, link:https://www.jenkins.io/[Jenkins]. The boring stuff nobody
notices until it breaks.

This blog started in 2004, in French first, mostly English since. I write here
mostly because writing helps me get things out of my head and think further. The
pace has been way too low for my taste for years, and I keep trying to fix that :-)

I'm always happy to get counter-opinions. My email is below.
